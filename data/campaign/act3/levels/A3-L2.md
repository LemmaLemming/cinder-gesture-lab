## Confirmed level design

The [Act 3 concept](../../../../docs/ACT3_CONCEPT.md#a3-l2-living-forest--the-floor-remembers) and [canonical research level](../../../../docs/reference-library/act3/research/levels.json) define the objective: **cross the Wombflash-inspired grove and open its dry exit**. The player question is which rooted source to commit before the approach tightens. The nine-minute first-clear estimate is an unplaytested pacing target, not a timer, compulsory wait or reason to add waves.

The intended route is forest threshold → three broad root courts → cleared canopy slit. Immense trunks have simple, visible fixed bases. Required floor stays continuous, firm and dry enough for ordinary dashes; damp leaves and mist express the setting without introducing slippery movement. High canopy, eye-like bark, white shadows and falling pixels remain scenery outside active warning layers. A sun change preserves floor/collision, gestures and aiming, and deals no damage by itself.

The new decision follows L1's source priority: **bait a fixed rooted commitment, circle its footprint, then choose the low recovery knot or the Stalker's ordinary opening**. Removing the Latcher releases area pressure; prioritising the Stalker may preserve the more useful approach. Root growth cannot secretly move collision under the hero, chase a completed dash or create endless sprouts. Recovery must offer an ordinary-primary opening in either sun state, without required pickups or blast ammo.
