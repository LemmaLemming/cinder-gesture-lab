## Sun and enemy contracts

Branchspell/Alppain are game encounter-state labels. Preview the next footprint with silhouette/icon and countdown ticks; stop moving it at lock. Recognition, remaining dash cooldown and one normal escape dash must fit before activation. Change appearance/easier flank over fixed floor; no dynamic collision in the initial implementation, damage from sunlight, gesture remapping, aiming rotation or occupied-floor loss. Preserve the current safe pocket. A separate hazard requires its own warning/lock/active/recovery and visibly delayed activation if occupied; it cannot create a cage. L1 need not introduce an extra hazard merely because the global rule permits one.

C50 / A3-E1 Sunbound Stalker is an invented enemy. It braces and tracks only during a defined warning, locks one short lane with its crest, lunges once, then settles with a low ordinary-primary opening. Both states remain slashable; state changes the useful approach/window, not permission to attack. The bright lane in G18 is a lunge footprint, never a laser. Limit initial preparing attackers to two with staggered locks, body-clear routes and no repeated contact damage. Health/turning/acceleration/commitment/recovery are explicit unplaytested proposal data in [encounter JSON](../a3_l1_encounters.json); art frames cannot silently change them.

White shadows, pillar mirages, harmless reflections, watching ornament and crystals remain scenery with no damage/collider/collectible/threat warning merely because they appear significant. Sun disks, lake margins and floating/impossible architecture stay outside local combat geometry. Warning shapes must read above scenery/mist, and source/whole footprint/safe landing/recovery flank must share the actual portrait view. Foreground rocks and low-tree canopy require occlusion review or fading.
