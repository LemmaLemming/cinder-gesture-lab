# Whole-sequence replay response witness

`replay-witness-1` in [replay_witness.gd](../../scripts/combat/replay_witness.gd) is a pure bounded response proof over [immutable recorded sequence data](REPLAY_SEQUENCE.md). It neither reserves a threat nor consumes a capture, moves an apparition, applies damage or accepts a level. Compound scheduling and physical playback must separately enforce its assumptions before using the result.

```gdscript
var proof = witness.prove(scheduler, sequence_snapshot, response, context,
    at_lock, permitted_reservation_id)
```

`context` binds `world_root`, `source_epoch`, `generation`, and the `capture_collision_fingerprint` measured from the actual world at the capture barrier. Every fingerprint numeric value must equal the current measured value exactly; even a one-bit scalar change rejects. Use [exact tagged JSON](../../scripts/campaign/exact_json.gd) for transport rather than decimal JSON round trips. This immutable identity check has no contact tolerance. `response` uses the scheduler's existing actual actor/stats/cooldown/commitment, supported floors and finite escape/return directions. A fresh lock proof uses the actual scheduler clock and retains the complete authored locked lead. Only the scheduler's exact matching unarmed replay may be excluded at lock; the pure `replay_exchange_error` must prove its identity and an otherwise empty reservation table. Other preparing, active or recovery sources reject without pruning or cancellation callbacks.

The shared player's public current stats and response timers must match. A pending weapon change rejects, rather than guessing the future loadout. Unlocked linear axes, the fixed shared capsule and actual scenery collision are required. The proof includes recognition, current commitment and dash cooldown, one complete ordinary dash before playback, and a second distinct ordinary dash for a two-combination sequence. Its second departure also retains normal dash cooldown and the first echo's complete safe ending. It checks every recorded primary and independently aimed optional blast together. Recorded harmless dash travel causes no attack. No blast ammo, damage absorption, immunity or pickups are credited as a response.

The final path must reach the authored stationary tether with ordinary primary range and scenery LOS, retain the ordinary input margin and finish full selected primary recovery before the unchanged authored exposure ends. A remote or briefly exposed tether rejects even when both recorded attacks can be escaped. The finite direction search and conservative full cone geometry may reject a route that another arrangement would allow; a failed recording needs a visible safe rejection/re-arm, never translated routes or abbreviated warnings.

## Actual dispatch timing

Recorded attacks are instantaneous executed actions. Their replay may dispatch on the next physics tick. The mandatory shared `MAX_DISPATCH_DELAY_S = 0.05` therefore makes every attack footprint dangerous continuously from its canonical deadline through deadline plus 0.05 seconds during the proof. This bound is provisional supported execution tolerance; it does not change captured spacing, player cooldowns or animation timing. The physical consumer must check **all newly due events** before delivering any and cancel the exchange without damage if any event is later than that bound. A nominal-instant-only witness cannot license late damage.

The next echo's safe beginning and final tether opening account for the latest possible dispatch plus the ordinary timing skin. Authored tether end remains fixed; a late tail shrinks or rejects the opening instead of creating extra recovery. Paused simulation time must not age this interval. The cursor's data-only ability to return old overdue events does not authorize their physical execution.

## Geometry and numerical scope

Live escape/return paths retain the scheduler's complete 0.32m shared capsule radius plus 0.01m safety skin, continuous analytic static-box floor support, actual capsule sweeps and safe wait/landing checks. Full recorded attack cones remain dangerous even where scenery might block their eventual damage; this is conservative. Admission also requires the complete current collision fingerprint to match the independently captured world. This is restricted contact evidence for an already recorded route, not proof that a new physical actor could travel that smaller capsule.

All live/captured positions and conservatively bounded collider extents must remain within ±512m; scheduler time stays within one million seconds. Static supported shapes and stable world identities are required. New walls, changed geometry, support gaps, unsupported axis locks or out-of-envelope dimensions fail closed. Dynamic floors, moving scenery, arbitrary sliding, unbounded coordinates and future gear/stat changes are outside this proof. Parent encounters must prevent unreserved attackers and provide actual portrait/loadout validation.

Accepted output is defensive diagnostic data with the timed path, ordinary-primary position/time, completion clock, canonical origin, sequence identity, profile/world guard, event count and mandatory dispatch bound. It explicitly reports no reservation, blast use or immunity. It is not an authorization token that a caller can forge and give to the scheduler; admission and lock each run the proof themselves.
