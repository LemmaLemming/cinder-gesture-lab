# Equipment balance research

Status: research support for Cinder's proposed mechanics and equipment guidelines. Accessed: **2026-10-08**.

## Evidence boundaries

All six sources are developer or publisher material. This file uses the official article or patch notes only; forum replies are excluded. The sources describe **historical changes or proposed changes**, not verified current game rules. They support design principles, not a claim that another game's balance transfers to Cinder.

Each `SRC` identifier is stable. `Evidence` records a source-supported claim; `Cinder implication` records an original recommendation. Implications are proposals until adopted by the canonical Cinder design. Source dates below reproduce the dates displayed on their pages without inferring a timezone.

## SRC-01 — Hades: The High Speed Update Patch Notes

- Publisher: Supergiant Games.
- [Source](https://www.supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-the-high-speed-update-patch-notes/).
- Publication date displayed: 2019-06-14; relevant Patch 022 scope: 2019-06-11.
- Accessed: 2026-10-08.
- Evidence: Power Shot damage bonuses and damage-reduction effects became additive. Two Rail upgrades became mutually exclusive. Some Cast boons were withheld when incompatible with the player's Cast.
- Cinder implication: Define stacking groups and incompatibilities explicitly. A separate final defense cap is a Cinder proposal; this patch does not establish a safe uncapped formula.

## SRC-02 — Game Balance in Path of Exile: Expedition

- Publisher: Grinding Gear Games; official Community Team post.
- [Source](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3147157).
- Publication date displayed: 2021-07-20.
- Accessed: 2026-10-08.
- Evidence: GGG described multiplicative damage supports crowding out utility choices and excessive damage preventing bosses from using abilities. Its damage-support budget gave larger damage benefits to stronger restrictions or costs.
- Cinder implication: Price utility, burst, and safety alongside sustained damage. Validate that an item's downside actually constrains its intended build, rather than assuming every written penalty is effective.

## SRC-03 — Development Manifesto: Game Balance in Path of Exile: Harvest

- Publisher: Grinding Gear Games; official Bex_GGG post.
- [Source](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2873295).
- Publication date displayed: 2020-06-15.
- Accessed: 2026-10-08.
- Evidence: GGG identified flat added damage as favoring high attack speed. Slow weapons were intended to retain commitment risk while receiving adequate rewards. Temporary boss stun immunity prevented repeated stuns removing encounter threat; a self-scaling speed effect received a maximum.
- Cinder implication: Evaluate attack cadence, per-hit triggers, burst and stagger together. Cap generated effects and prevent permanent boss control. Matching nominal DPS alone does not establish weapon parity.

## SRC-04 — Dead Cells: Update of Plenty

- Publisher: official Dead Cells patch notes.
- [Source](https://deadcells.com/patchnotes/19).
- Verified patch scope: Update 19.0–19.7, 2020-05-28 through 2020-07-06.
- Accessed: 2026-10-08.
- Evidence: Damage mutations changed from flat bonus DPS to percentage damage to address progression scaling. Status-synergy affixes became less ubiquitous; some self-enabling status bonuses were subsequently prohibited. Tonic was reworked into a cooldown effect providing temporary health instead of healing.
- Cinder implication: Give conditional synergies a deliberate setup cost. Define temporary-shield duration and cooldown. Percentage bonuses still require caps and combination tests; this change does not prove unlimited percentage stacking is balanced.

## SRC-05 — Dead Cells: Boss Rush

- Publisher: official Dead Cells patch notes.
- [Source](https://deadcells.com/patchnotes/31).
- Verified patch scope: Update 31.0–31.4, 2022-09-19 through 2022-10-10.
- Accessed: 2026-10-08.
- Evidence: Glyphs of Peril extended its combo as health fell, with a finite hit limit. Taunt made enemies faster and more vulnerable to melee damage. A later patch reduced the shield generated by an on-critical effect.
- Cinder implication: Bound low-health rewards and test them with speed, damage and shields. Cinder's three-strike idea needs a precise trigger and reset rule, rather than an ambiguous triple-damage promise.

## SRC-06 — Path of Exile 3.16 Balance: Part 2 — Core Character Defences and Recovery

- Publisher: Grinding Gear Games; official Community Team post.
- [Source](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3185101).
- Publication date displayed: 2021-10-07.
- Accessed: 2026-10-08.
- Evidence: GGG sought to strengthen accessible core defenses while reducing unusually cheap defensive outliers. It described cohesive defensive equipment modifiers and proposed hybrid recovery limited by the defensive stat invested in. The article explicitly presented plans subject to testing.
- Cinder implication: Keep clothing slot identity readable and conversion effects bounded. An armour-to-health speed ratio should use maximum health, with explicit limits; that ratio is original to Cinder, not sourced here.

## Numerical tuning limits

- These sources do **not** establish Cinder's health total, armour multiplier, damage ceiling, dash length, dash duration, attack timings, item budgets, buff durations or combo multiplier.
- Any such value in Cinder's guidelines is a **provisional tuning hypothesis**. Attribute its motivation to a source where useful; do not attribute the number itself without direct evidence.
- Research-supported design principles do not mean the resulting equipment is already balanced. Cinder must test its actual gesture controller, close-range weapons, encounters and combined loadouts.
- Cinder's identical core mechanics across three acts are a user requirement. The cited games do not establish that requirement. Equipment changes should be evaluated against it.
- A cap or additive formula is a guardrail, not proof of fairness. Validate sustained output, short opening burst, reach, incoming damage, mobility and encounter success together.
