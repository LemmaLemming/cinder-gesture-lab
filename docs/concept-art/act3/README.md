## Three possible game views

1. [Twin Suns](gameplay/01-twin-suns-gameplay.png): familiar ground dashes and close attacks under a new light-state rule.
2. [Mirror Sea](gameplay/02-mirror-sea-gameplay.png): a dry shore, one committed enemy replay and a useful safe landing.
3. [Crystalman](gameplay/03-crystalman-gameplay.png): a player-action echo and a low rooted tether offering the next opening.

These preserve portrait format, close fixed-angle overhead camera, pixel sprites against blocky 2.5D scenery, normal ground dashes, one carried weapon and close-range attacks. Act 1 supplies level/checkpoint/optional-level scale; this act has its own scenery and palette.

## Research and visual boundaries

Read [ADAPTATION_NOTES.md](ADAPTATION_NOTES.md) before using a board as source reconstruction or implementing its diagrams. [ACT3_CONCEPT.md](../../ACT3_CONCEPT.md) and [levels.json](../../reference-library/act3/research/levels.json) govern combat. The full cast is indexed in the database; character boards prioritize visual references rather than assigning every named person a sprite.

Two suns, white shadows, extra colours, changing organs, immense forest, strange water and false-world revelation inform these studies. Exact architecture, garment cuts, flesh/eye motifs, arenas and combat are proposals. Violet/sulphur-green cannot literally reproduce ulfire/jale. Optional spaces reuse parent scenery, terrain, enemies and effects with few additions.

Sullenbode's Garden is an external root network projecting a non-damageable likeness; the finale opens a local escape from Crystalman's world. These are invented encounters, not a claim to recreate the novel's events or definitively defeat its underlying reality.
