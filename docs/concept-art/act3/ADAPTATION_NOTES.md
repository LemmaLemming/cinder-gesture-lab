# Reading the Act 3 concept collection

All generated art is a **game proposal**. The database separately labels novel facts, interpretations and game proposals. Use the art's silhouettes/materials/composition for exploration and the database's checked chapter evidence for source citation.

## Source details

- Branchspell is white and Alppain blue. Violet/green and pixel patterns are representational, not literal ulfire/jale.
- The arrival desert has scarlet sand and black-stem purple shrubs. Shaping's desert well is dark green; Poolingdred's pure gnawl water is crystal clear. Iron-red water belongs to Crimtyphon's clearing. Clear-water basins in revised arrival art are staged gnawl-water interpretations, not a literal reconstruction of Shaping's desert shrine.
- Wombflash has widely spaced immense trunks, mist, wet fallen leaves and no underbrush in Chapter 13. Panawe's Chapter 7 recollection describes dark red trunks/pale ulfire foliage. Fleshy bark, eyes, root enemies and violet pixel shimmer are additions.
- Maskull is broad/robust, with black beard and short bristling hair. The game's ivory-coated protagonist is a different traveller.
- Polecrab is bald, short, sturdy and wrinkled with three eyes; Gleameil has yellow hair and three eyes; Dreamsinter is enormous with three luminous eyes, black coiled hair and staff. Earthrid's source instrument is Lake Irontick; instrument frames in art are visual analogies.
- Leehallfae is a third-sex phaen, with ae/aer/aerself pronouns, luminous copper skin, turban and very long plait. Pale treatments are palette/value proposals. Sullenbode's maroon hair/luminous skin inform the nonhostile likeness. Krag/Surtur is a harsh liberating guide, not the evil final boss.

## Gameplay boundaries

Sunbound Stalker's short line is a committed **lunge lane**, even if a drawing resembles a ray. Root Latcher owns a bounded fixed footprint. Mirror Echo previews **its own** dash/slash; recording the player begins only at Crystalman. Reflections are decorative.

The targets are low external roots/tethers on permanent broad ground. Narrow bridge sketches do not establish required traversal. There are no compulsory jumps, swimming, vehicles, climbing or boosted gaps. Sun changes and stripped scenery preserve safe floor and confirmed gestures. White shadows remain decorative; attack warnings use distinct outlines/timing.

Crystalman captures actual completed world-space movement and attack footprints, keeping the player's screen-space aiming anchor separate. Safe-route, cooldown and local-camera checks precede lock. One or two finite combinations play sequentially without other hazards or recursive recording. Ordinary dash/slash with fixed damage can clear every required encounter; organs/pickups are optional tactical variants.

## Text and production limits

Headings and poetic captions are generated design notes, not verified Lindsay dialogue. Revisions remove source-attributed invented quotes where detected. The checked research files and original chapters are the citation authority. Art diagrams/typography do not define a production interface.

Exact hitboxes, scale, timing, collision, camera occlusion and performance require later playable work. [The full prompt manifest](generated-manifest.json) preserves initial and revision instructions; the gallery verifies selected local files independently of proposed study-slot counts.
