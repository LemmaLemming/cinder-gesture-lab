# Act 1 environment boards

Five original development boards cover the full required journey: [Observatory and Launch](01-observatory-and-launch.png), [Crater Gardens](02-crater-gardens.png), [Mushroom Caverns](03-mushroom-caverns.png), [Selenite Court](04-selenite-court.png), and [The Living Moon and return](05-living-moon-and-return.png).

The boards expand the six earlier researched studies while preserving their handmade theatre, painted flats, finless capsule, period expedition clothes and monochrome game palette. Each proposes broad ground floors for ordinary any-angle dashes, a locally readable encounter, and reusable scenery modules. The three side levels reuse their parent kit with few additions. Small diagrams are illustrative, not measured floor plans; exact collision, camera and enemy timing are governed by [ACT1_CONCEPT.md](../../../ACT1_CONCEPT.md).

The mushroom board replaces the earlier automatic-vent study with low reachable clusters: hitting one makes spores fall and temporarily repel living enemies. Court ornament animation and the Moon's face-side arena are game inventions. Earth board lunar flats and decorative costume details are stylized game proposals, not film observations. Captions and extra ornamental lettering are generated labels, not source quotations.

Complete prompts, reference-image paths and related database IDs are recorded in the [manifest](../generated-manifest.json). These are planning images rather than production tiles, sprites or implemented levels.
