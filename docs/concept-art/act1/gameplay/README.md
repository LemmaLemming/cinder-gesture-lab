# Three possible Act 1 game views

These original portrait concepts illustrate the intended game camera and encounter readability. They are generated mockups, not captures of the current Godot prototype.

1. [Crater Gardens](01-crater-gardens-gameplay.png): one committed Rush Selenite, a clear side landing, and a prominent period traveller.
2. [Mushroom repulsion](02-mushroom-spores-gameplay.png): the player hits a reachable cluster; falling spores make living upright swarmers retreat and open floor space.
3. [Moon-eye recovery](03-moon-eye-gameplay.png): a local eye rim lowers to the floor for a close slash during recovery beside a broad safe clearing.

The camera looks down diagonally at a continuous floor, with close player-follow framing, upright pixel sprites and blocky scenery. Combat retains one carried weapon, baseline damage, any-angle swipe-only dashes, immediate tap slash and a quick second-tap blast. Aiming reads the final screen-space release endpoint of the latest swipe. No joystick or new interaction control is proposed.

No HUD is shown so the scene and silhouettes can be assessed clearly. [ACT1_CONCEPT.md](../../../ACT1_CONCEPT.md) governs mechanics, checkpoint rules and learning; art does not verify collision, scale or touch usability. Full prompts and reference inputs are in the [manifest](../generated-manifest.json).
