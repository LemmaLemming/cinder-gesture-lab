Render-only helper now used by the authored A3-L3 vignette candidate; native arrival construction passes, portrait review remains pending. [G07 shore/ground kit](../../concept-art/act3/environments/03-mirror-sea.png), [G17 crystal shrub/dry stone kit](../../concept-art/act3/props/02-journey-and-modules.png) and [G19 close game view](../../concept-art/act3/gameplay/02-mirror-sea-gameplay.png) were opened directly. They suggest broad broken strata, low pale slab faces and sparse branching mineral at the margins. They remain concept references; no painting or board is loaded as a runtime atlas. Follow the [reuse guide](../../ASSET_REUSE_GUIDE.md), [style guide](../../GAME_STYLE_GUIDELINES.md) and [adaptation boundaries](../../concept-art/act3/ADAPTATION_NOTES.md).

[CinderAct3MirrorShoreModules](../../../scripts/acts/act3/mirror_shore_modules.gd) is an independent `Node3D`. Add an empty instance under the untransformed actual World, then call `build(stable_court_id, court_z)`. The root remains at `(0,0,court_z)`; all pieces retain fixed court-relative placement. Repeating the same build succeeds without adding pieces; changing a built court is refused. `state()` reports native mesh bounds and readiness only. It grants no floor, camera or gameplay authority.
