## Why retain this exercise

The [canonical garden design](../../ACT3_CONCEPT.md#a3-l4-false-paradise--the-cost-of-staying) already asks the player to choose pressure removal versus tether progress. Phase1 separately teaches Draw and Enclose. A genuine 60→30 target boundary introduces the familiar sun preview and one removable helper; it never respawns after a real defeat. A survivor can be retired alive at final release, preserving the option to prioritize the tether. This composition supplies a distinct target/action/return decision without another arrangement swap.

The [accepted handoff](A3_L4_FOCUSED_HANDOFF.md) reports Root120/Echo95 on Shared49 and Root/Sun138 on Shared50, including living-helper retirement and inspected held/released portraits. It explicitly leaves the helper-dead branch unexecuted. Those originals retain their actual source/baseline/diagnostic scopes. They support the reason for a small next check; they are not current Challenge helper-removal evidence or proof of a difficulty increase.

Retention is justified only if a bounded native comparison demonstrates both options remain useful: direct tether progress preserves actual optional pressure, while ordinary helper removal spends an opening/approach and removes that pressure without resetting target HP or preventing a return. Failure of that hypothesis returns to Integration for a revised proposal, rather than silently adding geometry or weakening safety rules. Coverage/exception acceptance remains Integration's decision.
