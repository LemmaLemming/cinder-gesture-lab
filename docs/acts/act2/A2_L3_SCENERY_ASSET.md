# A2-L3 Ruined House scenery kit — initial production

Original collisionless scenery for **Black Smoke and the Ruined House — Under the Machines**. The Act2 owner supplies actual floor/bank collision, the central blocker, smoke, encounters, progression and the saved clock. This kit supplies an exposed kitchen/scullery/pantry, a quiet dry route and outboard industrial work.

**Readiness:** authored, source reviewed and statically checked. No engine import, native portrait, measured mesh-bound/camera proof, full-level encounter, checkpoint or acceptance result yet. Source boards remain references; no full board or crop is used as a runtime atlas. Previous Heath/Weybridge tests do not establish readiness of this new arrangement.

## Source and adaptation

Visually inspected [G07 Black Smoke / Ruined House](../../concept-art/act2/environments/black-smoke-ruined-house.png), SHA256 `35070da144b4ae8f64916c9b568685a7fd2744f1eb8526ae6b11dd37f0a59fcb`; [G19 ruined-house gameplay](../../concept-art/act2/gameplay/02-ruined-house-gameplay.png), SHA256 `e38938995714e40b70340dc3b2f8725d5249d1ee6b55ac819d4a82de18d84d81`; and [sample S02 Handling Machine counter](../../concept-art/act2/gameplay/sample-screenshots/02-handling-machine-counter.png), SHA256 `da547d49a74749f809824867bf1496ff6019697f18373c9e4b3d7fcd0a0ffd9e`. Sample S02 is the gameplay image, distinct from research-source S02 (the same Gutenberg novel edition as research S01).

G07 supplies the opened house, pantry vessels, brick openings, exposed timber and excavated industrial ground. G19 supplies dry worn house flooring and broken edge masonry. Sample S02 supplies the broad dry work floor, machinery beside the route and clear approaches around a reachable machine. Read the [L3 concept](../../ACT2_CONCEPT.md#a2-l3-black-smoke-and-the-ruined-house--under-the-machines), [selected-art adaptation boundaries](../../concept-art/act2/README.md), category/generation/sample notes and [research entities](../../reference-library/act2/research/entities.json): E20 kitchen/scullery/pantry and E21 industrial excavation are the source basis. The wide continuous game floor, removed front wall and small harmless work stand are explicit gameplay/presentation adaptations, not a literal novel floor plan or a new Martian caste.

The rust/black/pale-ash material family reuses the original [Heath helpers](../../../scripts/acts/act2/heath_kit.gd) and [Weybridge helpers](../../../scripts/acts/act2/weybridge_kit.gd) read-only. Brick, charred timber, oxidised metal, rivets, rings, sand and stone keep those deterministic textures and primitive mesh caches. Five new original64×64 nearest materials supply low-contrast ash, worn plank flooring, chipped plaster, dull ceramic and earthen pottery. Their broad clustered marks are generated by integer formulas without random state or shader time; ceramic and fasteners have no emissive interaction glow.

## Parent interface and material custody

Script: [ruined_house_kit.gd](../../../scripts/acts/act2/ruined_house_kit.gd), `extends RefCounted`.

- `static build(parent: Node3D) -> Dictionary` creates one named `RuinedHouseSceneryKit` child and returns `{root: Node3D, witnesses: Array[Node3D], work_tableau: Node3D}`. `witnesses` is empty: this occupied excavation introduces no new civilian/faction tableau.
- `static set_work_tableau(kit: Dictionary, active: bool, progress: float = 0.0) -> void` reconstructs the plate, clamp, two rods and moving journal from finite progress clamped0…1 (nonfinite becomes0). `false,0` is initial quiet work; `false,1` retains the delivered arrangement quietly. `active` only closes the decorative clamp slightly; it creates no event, deadline or authority. Parent calls from its owned scenic clock and uses the same fields for quiet restore.
- The kit root's `optional_cutaway_paths` contains mesh paths relative to **the kit root**, with the existing `weybridge_cutaway_candidate` metadata on every non-floor mesh. The level derives temporary alpha from its actual camera/hero/cue/landing requirements; metadata supplies eligibility only.
- Every mesh owns a separate `StandardMaterial3D` override before it is marked for cutaway. Cached deterministic textures and immutable primitive meshes remain shared. No pose mutates a cached mesh/material or allocates new resources. Floor materials own their UV scaling and never enter optional cutaway paths.
- Root metadata records art revision, source IDs, scenery-only role and expected central-wall bounds; named prop roots carry stable asset IDs. No process/physics method, timer, random generation, signal, shader `TIME`, shared consumer or player-system modification is introduced.

## Deferred source features and review limits

The G07 dense black-smoke banks/canister impacts, panoramic industrial pit and skyline, full house walls/roof and source captivity are not implemented here. Shared smoke production is pending and remains root-owned. There is no steam clearing, water/red weed, ecological hazard, novel crowd faction, private escape proof or bright actionable prop cue. Boss/Tender/Handler production and mechanic phases are separate actors/consumers.

The geometry is source-informed production, not finished portrait acceptance. Root must verify side-room/pottery/arch visibility at the close following camera, actual low-wall collision/approaches, floor/cue contrast and derived cutaway over hero/safe landings. Outboard decoration can be deliberately cropped; small ceramic rims/rivets may merge and the tall side rafters may need stronger cutaway at some viewpoints. Analytical clear widths/outboard limits above do not replace actual all-mesh/current-camera review. No future optional-level or complete-board readiness is claimed.
