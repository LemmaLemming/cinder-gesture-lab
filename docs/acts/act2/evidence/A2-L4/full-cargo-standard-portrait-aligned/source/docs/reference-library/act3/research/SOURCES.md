## Audit and exclusions

Names were checked across all chapters, including Panawe’s memories, Gleameil’s legend, Haunte’s Lodd anecdote and the Earth séance. Proper names that are merely cultural references or scenery labels are excluded from the living cast. The database makes no claim that the novel’s five primary colors, twin-sun light, back rays or physiological transformations are current scientific facts.

No original published cover or later adaptation artwork is used as a canonical design source. Generated concept sheets are new interpretations and carry `game_proposal`; visual departures should be recorded in the art manifest/readme. The limited date/edition metadata is bibliographic context, not a claim that the novel is newly revised by its author.
