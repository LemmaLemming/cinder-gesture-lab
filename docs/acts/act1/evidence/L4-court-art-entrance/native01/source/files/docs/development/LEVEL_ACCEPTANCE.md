# Desktop level acceptance

Policy: **focused-desktop-level-1**, effective 9 October 2026 by direct user instruction. This supersedes earlier requirements for agent-completed level or campaign playthroughs wherever those requirements appear in the objective, development guidance or act records.

> Agents do not need to play through or beat each level or the campaign. Use focused automated checks for mechanics, loading, transitions, saves, retry and cleanup, plus brief visual inspections.

A full route, defeating every enemy or reaching the exit is not required before accepting a level and starting the next. The fifteen-main/nine-optional desktop campaign scope, authored content, progression rules, shared controls, ownership and canonical equipment/ability ledgers remain. Stop before mobile export and release work.

## Focused review

Use the changed level's relevant automated checks and directly affected shared checks. Reuse existing evidence for unchanged behavior; no blanket rerun of accepted levels or all legal-kit playthroughs is required. A focused fixture may arrange a legitimate representative state and exercise the real production interface without claiming that an agent earned that state through a playthrough.

| Category | Evidence to record |
| --- | --- |
| Mechanics | Focused checks of the level's authored interactions and relevant shared control/cue behavior, with actual results and scope. |
| Loading | Scene and shared-shell entry, stable level identity and valid initial state. |
| Transitions | Real production completion/contact/next-level or optional/replay flow as applicable, exercised in a focused fixture. An earned route to the exit is unnecessary. |
| Saves | Coherent representative whole-level state and supported load/Continue behavior; no mixture of refreshed resources and old encounter state. |
| Retry | Supported checkpoint restoration and preserved story/replay separation. |
| Cleanup | Removal of the level's actors, reservations, cues, connections and temporary effects through normal disposal. |
| Brief visual inspection | Actual portrait view of representative content for floor contact, framing, cues, source/landing readability, occlusion and relevant presentation. No sequence of captures covering a completed route is required. |

The worker's handoff names the exact content commit, adopted shared baseline/API, scene/level ID, focused commands/results, visual evidence and limitations. Map existing evidence to these categories and explain an inapplicable category where appropriate. A failure in a focused category is investigated and corrected; a failed old route harness alone is not proof of a gameplay defect. Preserve its original diagnostics and distinguish test-script, environment and gameplay causes.

## Acceptance and next level

Integration reviews and accepts each level independently under this policy, records the exact commit/evidence and completes its production registration with focused checks. The worker then proceeds to the next assigned level in sequence. Do not wait for another act, a full-playthrough test, human balancing or mobile validation. Internal acceptance remains autonomous.

The eight levels accepted when this policy was issued remain accepted without requalification. The current total is10/24; later acceptances follow this policy. Pending levels require review of their focused evidence and brief visuals; this policy does not silently accept unfinished content. Optional completion remains independent of the main route and parent unlock rules remain tested through focused flows.

## Untested behavior and original evidence

Every current level record states whether end-to-end playthrough, defeat-all behavior, exit reached through ordinary play and full-campaign traversal were tested. Use **untested full-playthrough behavior** where no such result exists, and retain known concrete issues with their actual scope. These statements are limitations, not gates. Never relabel a failed, canceled or unexecuted route as passed.

Do not submit another long route run solely for the superseded gate. The owner may close an existing route job through its normal handle with cleanup and exact custody/closure records when its remaining work is no longer useful. Keep frozen inputs intact while a job holds or waits for the shared lock; never kill another worker's process, delete its lock or bypass the queue.

Canonical coordination records this policy in run.json. Integration is first help contact for all three workers. The original objective remains historical source material; this later user instruction takes precedence.
