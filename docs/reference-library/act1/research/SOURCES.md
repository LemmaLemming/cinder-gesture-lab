# Act I sources

Checked 8 October 2026. Direct image links and their licence metadata remain in the existing F/A/M/O catalogue; sources below support names, performer attributions, scene context and provenance. Descriptions are original paraphrases.

## S01 — Lobster Films — Le Voyage dans la Lune story tableaux

[Lobster Films](https://melies.lobsterfilms.com/contes.php) · Undated; accessed 2026-10-08 · restorer_story_guide

Film-restorer story guide; its 15 headings are a retelling, not a verified count of original 1902 tableaux. Supports character names and event labels. Alcofribras is the spelling here; alternate spellings retained.

## S02 — Cinémathèque française — surviving astronomer costume

[La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/objet/392.html) · 1902 costume · museum_object

Original robe in the first two tableaux; distinguishes its trim from Barbenfouillis’s near-identical robe and identifies Méliès as Barbenfouillis.

## S03 — Cinémathèque française — Labisse Selenite reconstruction

[La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/objet/1540.html) · 1960 reconstruction · museum_reconstruction

Later cardboard reconstruction after a 1937 drawing; not an original 1902 suit, material or colour record. Museum identifies Folies Bergère acrobats as Selenite performers.

## S04 — Cinémathèque française — Les Sélénites drawing

[La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/objet/400.html) · 1937 · retrospective_drawing

Retrospective drawing; existing M06/A04 preserve date and reproduction uncertainty.

## S05 — MoMA — The Dream drawing

[The Museum of Modern Art](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/38324) · c.1930–31 · retrospective_drawing

Later scene drawing; catalogue title includes Phoebus while the restorer’s retelling names Phoebe. Keep that title discrepancy; do not rename the visible crescent woman from the later title.

## S06 — MoMA — Giant Mushroom Grotto drawing

[The Museum of Modern Art](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/38318) · c.1930–31 · retrospective_drawing

Retrospective grotto drawing; a variation on film staging rather than a verified 1902 set plan.

## S07 — MoMA — lunar landing and Earth drawing

[The Museum of Modern Art](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/36891) · c.1930–31 · retrospective_drawing

Later landing/Earth scene; title calls the vehicle a rocket shell. Visual game specification remains the film’s finless bullet-shaped shell.

## S08 — MoMA — Moon-eye drawing

[The Museum of Modern Art](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/38327) · c.1930–31 · retrospective_drawing

Retrospective Moon-eye drawing, existing M01; not preproduction art.

## S09 — Cinémathèque française — Georges Méliès par ses archives

[Tania Capron / La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/article/1881.html) · 2022-08-30 · institutional_archive_essay

Distinguishes original design records from later recompositions and describes theatrical staging. Supports provenance method, not invented boss mechanics.

## S10 — MoMA — To Save and Project programme announcement

[The Museum of Modern Art](https://press.moma.org/wp-content/files_mf/tsap_pressrelease_final.pdf) · 2011 · institutional_restoration_context

Programme documents the restored hand-coloured version and the restoration partners. Monochrome remains this project’s art direction.

## S11 — Ciclic — Le Voyage dans la Lune teacher guide

[Ciclic](https://ciclic.fr/sites/default/files/fichiers/livret-enseignant.pdf) · 2013 publication metadata; film 1902 · institutional_education_guide

Search-index extract checked for cast: Méliès [Barbenfouillis], Bleuette Bernon [Phoebe], François Lallement [officer], Henri Delannoy [pilot]. Full PDF did not open in this session; these performer-role assignments are published attributions, not independently verified identities in stills.

## S12 — BFI Player — A Trip to the Moon

[British Film Institute](https://player.bfi.org.uk/free/film/watch-a-trip-to-the-moon-1902-online) · 1902 film · institutional_film_record

Institutional film record and viewing entry. Does not supply individual cast identities for every visible performer.

## S13 — Georges Méliès — film frames and production photographs in the existing library

[Georges Méliès; reproductions served by Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Le_Voyage_dans_la_Lune) · 1902 film; reproduction/version dates vary · primary_film_imagery

Primary visual evidence is each linked F01–F13/A01–A17/USER01 record, with its direct Commons page and credit. Category link is only an entry point. No new archival pixels were downloaded; visual descriptions inherit specific legacy reference URLs.

## S14 — Cinémathèque française — Le Voyage dans la Lune film record and full cast

[La Cinémathèque française](https://www.cinematheque.fr/film/52565.html) · 1902 film; current institutional record · institutional_film_cast

Full published cast credits checked. Retains surnames Brunnet, Delpierre, Farjaux and Kelm as given; no mapping from those actor names to the five story-guide companion names is established. Runtime metadata varies by presentation, so it does not set game-level duration.

## S15 — Festival de Cannes — Le Voyage dans la Lune, Cannes Classics 2011

[Festival de Cannes](https://www.festival-cannes.com/f/le-voyage-dans-la-lune/) · 2011 restored presentation; film production 1902 · institutional_restoration_film_record

Official production year, six-traveller synopsis and four performer/role credits; the listed AIR score belongs to the modern restoration presentation, not a verified 1902 original score.
