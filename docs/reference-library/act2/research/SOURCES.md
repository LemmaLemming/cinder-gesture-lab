The full source metadata is in [sources.json](sources.json). [entities.json](entities.json) gives Book I/II chapter numbers and titles for each record. These chapter references are more stable than a browser’s temporary line numbering.

Useful primary-text sections:

- **Book I, Chapters 1–6:** normal life, astronomical reports, Horsell spectators, named scientists, anatomy at first emergence and the first Heat-Ray massacre.
- **Book I, Chapters 8–13:** early military deployment, Maybury evacuation, flexible tripods, artillery survivor, Weybridge/Shepperton, the curate and ruined river country.
- **Book I, Chapters 14–17:** delayed news, failed defences, Black Smoke, the brother and Elphinstone women, distinct exodus figures, naval evacuation and Thunder Child.
- **Book II, Chapters 1–4:** ruined house, five-legged handling-machine, anatomical explanation, red plants, industrial manufacture, captives and the probing arm.
- **Book II, Chapters 5–8:** escape, red-weed flooding and disease, the artilleryman’s imagined underground future, empty London and the final redoubt’s microbial collapse.
- **Book II, Chapters 9–10:** kindness, recovery, reunion, scientific uncertainty and continuing trauma.

The novel is public domain. Research descriptions are paraphrases; the database does not reproduce long passages. Generated art and all gestures, damage windows, enemy role names and level timings are project proposals, not primary-source illustrations or implemented gameplay.
